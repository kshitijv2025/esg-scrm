# DISCOVERY: Response rate vs coverage rate — the metric that matters

## What

The brief targets 60% supplier response rate. The value auditor found the correct metric is coverage rate: what fraction of total procurement spend is covered by responding suppliers.

## Detail

If the 60% who respond are small suppliers (trims, packaging, logistics) while the 40% who don't respond include the large spinning mills and dye houses (60% of procurement spend), Scope 3 data is useless. The non-responding 40% represents the emissions that actually matter.

## Implication

Validate with a 3-supplier pilot before scaling. Target the largest 3 suppliers by spend first — if they respond, the coverage is high even at lower response rates.
