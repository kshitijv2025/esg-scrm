# Gap: Team Section Missing From All Deliverables

**Date**: 2026-05-17
**Type**: GAP
**Severity**: HIGH

## Finding

The Team & Operations section of the business plan has almost zero content. The only team references across 15+ deliverables are:

1. VC critique: "three MBA students pitching a data engineering product with no technical founder"
2. Founder correction: "we have a technical co-founder who has shipped enterprise SaaS before"
3. Investor requirement: "4-year vesting, 1-year cliff, signed before wire"

No team bios, roles, org chart, hiring plan, advisory board, or operations plan exist in any document.

## Why It Matters

This was the VC's **first critique** in the pitch analysis and it remains unaddressed. For a business plan that investors will share with partners, the team section is often the most-read section after the executive summary. Its absence will be noticed immediately.

## What's Needed

- Founding team bios with relevant experience
- Technical co-founder credentials (enterprise SaaS shipping history)
- Key hires planned for first 6 months (2 backend, 1 frontend, 1 WhatsApp/AI, 1 sales)
- Advisory board (ESG compliance expert, Big 4 audit partner, Bangladesh market connector)
- Legal structure and company location
- Organizational chart

## Blocker

Cannot be completed by AI — requires actual team member details, bios, and professional history from the founders.
