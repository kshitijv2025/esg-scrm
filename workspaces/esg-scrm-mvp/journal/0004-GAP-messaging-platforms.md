# GAP: Messaging platform split needs correction

## What

The brief says: Bangladesh → WhatsApp, Vietnam/Thailand → LINE, India/Indonesia → WhatsApp.

Reality:

- LINE has no market presence in Bangladesh (Facebook Messenger + Pathao dominate)
- WeChat requires Chinese business license (impossible for Bangladesh factory's suppliers)
- Correct split: Bangladesh/India/Indonesia → WhatsApp. Thailand → LINE only. China → deferred to v2.

## Additional Detail

WhatsApp Business API burst limit: 20 messages/minute. Sending questionnaires to 200 suppliers simultaneously triggers Meta spam detection → account temporarily banned. Must throttle sends over 10+ minutes.

Meta Business Verification: 2–8 weeks. Cannot send any WhatsApp messages until verified.

## Implication

Brief needs factual correction before next investor presentation. Messaging platform strategy is viable but the market-specific split is wrong.
