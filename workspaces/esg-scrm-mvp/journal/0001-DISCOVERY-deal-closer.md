# DISCOVERY: Feature 2 is the deal-closer, not Feature 1

## What

The value auditor and all three analysts independently reached the same conclusion through different paths.

## Detail

Feature 1 (multi-framework mapping) is received as "nice to have" by the Bangladesh factory CFO. They have a workaround (manual Excel). The buyer who says "yes first" is responding to H&M's Scope 3 contract requirement — Feature 2.

Feature 2 (WhatsApp supplier questionnaires for Scope 3) is a revenue-protection decision. H&M told the supplier: "Give us Scope 3 data in 90 days or we reconsider your preferred-supplier status." That's $100-140M of revenue at risk. The CFO will sign to protect that, not to get better CSRD disclosures.

## Implication

The investor pitch and product positioning should lead with Feature 2, not Feature 1. The one-liner is not "we map CSRD to GRI." It's "H&M just told your supplier they need Scope 3 data in 90 days. We solve that."

## Source

Value auditor (all 4 agents independently confirmed)
