# DECISION: Pricing confirmed at $4,000–5,000/month

## What

VC red team challenged the $2,000/month price point as too low to support SaaS unit economics with three engineers. Founders confirmed $4-5K/month is supportable based on value-to-customer math.

## Detail

A factory protecting $40M in H&M revenue has 667–833x ROI at $48-60K/year. The price is not arbitrary SaaS pricing — it's the cost of not losing preferred-supplier status.

This changes:

- Five customers = $240-300K ARR (not $120K)
- SaaS unit economics work at this ACV
- The pitch should never use "$2K/month" again

## Why This Matters

The VC red team initially scored "pricing / unit economics" as a concern. Once $4-5K was confirmed, it landed as "Landed." This is the correct price for the investor deck.

## Source

VC red team Round 1-4, founder confirmation

## Action

Update all pitch documents: replace "$2,000/month" with "$4,000-5,000/month ($48-60K/year)".
